## When do you need a newborn nanny?
- The first weeks after birth.
- Dad going back to work, or family living far away.
- Link: [newborn and mother care at home](/en/services/newborn-care/)

## Day, night or live-in?
- Day shift 8am–8pm; night shift 8pm–8am.
- When a [night nanny for newborns](/en/services/night-nanny/) is the right fit.
- When a [live-in nanny](/en/services/live-in-nanny/) makes more sense.

## Questions to ask before you choose
- Are they background-checked?
- Would you prefer a male or female caregiver?
- Language: English and basic Arabic.
- Will the same person stay for the whole plan?

## What to prepare before day one
- Baby's essentials in one place, mum's instructions in writing, your paediatrician's number.
